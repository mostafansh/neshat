Good to know

  • About a minute per case

    Each project page says how many cases it has.

  • Pixels only

    Images come without names, ID numbers or scan headers.

  • Research only

    Your answers and their timing are recorded for the study. Not for clinical use.

How it works

  1. Choose a project

    Read its page, then agree to take part.

  2. Read each case

    Answer the project's question and say how sure you are. Then lock your answer. After that you may see a suggestion, and you may keep or change your answer.

  3. Finish the project

    Some details of the project are explained at its end.
